If you're a new user of this project, the first step is to sign up for an account. Once registered and logged in, you'll gain full access to the platform. You'll be able to browse a wide variety of home listings shared by other users.

As a registered user, you can also add your own home listings, allowing others to view and book your property. After adding a listing, you're free to edit or delete your home details at any time.

In addition, any logged-in user can leave reviews on home listings, helping others make informed choices. You're also able to edit or delete your reviews whenever needed.

This platform offers a seamless and interactive experience for travelers and hosts alike — with user-friendly tools for managing listings, feedback, and bookings, and much more to explore!
